There are different modes like 9 pin-no-tap: That is where, if you get 9 pins in the 1st part of the frame. History of Bowling

The earliest form of bowling known to exist has been traced back to ancient Egyptian time, around 5,000 B.C. The ancient Egyptians rolled stones at various objects with the goal of knocking them over. In about 1875 A.C. delegates from each of the 8 bowling center in the U.S. made the USBC (United States Bowling Congress) which is now known as the maker of bowling.



Different Tools

To bowl your going to need a ball to bowl with. If you are just playing for fun you can just use a house ball and it will just vary with the different weights of the balls. If your trying to go pro or just want to be in a league you’re going to want a personalized ball with a certain amount of spin on it or a coat of something on it such as a reactive resin, plastic or a urethane coat. They all vary, on if you want your ball to move at all, for example, the urethane eats up the oil on the lane and curves all over it to hit the pocket of your pins.



Different Ways to Bowl

There are many different ways to bowl such as how many hands you use or how many fingers that you put in the ball. You can use either 1 hand or 2 hands, 2 hands is mainly for massive amount of curving and 1 hand is used to either throw straight or curve a little. For a 2 hander you will want to have 1-3 holes in your ball for the best performance, and for a 1 hander you will want 3 holes for best performance but 4 and 5 holes is definitely doable.
